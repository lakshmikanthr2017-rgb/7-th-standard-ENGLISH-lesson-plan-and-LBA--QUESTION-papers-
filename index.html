7th Standard English Second Language | Unit 5: The Wonder Bowl

Part 1: 5E Lesson Plan Framework

Phase Teacher Activity & Classroom Process Learning Outcomes (LO) Addressed
Engage Show visual prompt of sharing meals. Ask: "Have you ever helped a stranger or shared your meal?" LO-11: Observes social situations and frames relevant questions.
Explore Read aloud paragraphs 1–4 of The Wonder Bowl. Highlight key vocabulary (mound, hooted, ragged). LO-3: Responds to literal and analytical questions.
Explain Discuss hospitality, altruism, and grammar concepts (Adverbs and Adjectives). LO-36 / LO-38: Applies grammatical structures accurately.
Elaborate Connect story themes to real-life community values and kindness in school. LO-13: Supports viewpoints with logical reasons.
Evaluate Conduct formative assessment using oral questions and written LBA items. LO-1 / LO-26: Demonstrates reading comprehension.

Part 2: 20-Marks LBA Question Paper & Answer Key

Section A: Written Assessment (15 Marks)

I. Choose the correct alternative (MCQs):

  1. Philemon and Baucis lived in a __ on a mound.
    A) mansion    B) hut    C) palace    D) castle
  2. What did Baucis place on the table for the ragged strangers?
    A) Cup of tea    B) Small bowl of milk    C) Loaf of bread    D) Bowl of rice
  3. When guests drank from the bowl, Baucis was astonished because:
    A) It emptied    B) It refilled with milk as much as wanted    C) It broke    D) It turned to water
  4. The opposite of the word 'earlier' is:
    A) sooner    B) faster    C) later    D) quicker
  5. What was the final wish asked by old Philemon and Baucis?
    A) Rich merchants    B) Bigger farm    C) To die together    D) Travel world

II. Short Answers (3 Marks):
6. Why did the village boys hoot at the strangers?
7. What did Philemon and Baucis conclude that the strangers were?
8. What did the poor cottage turn into by the end of the story?

III. Paragraph Answers (4 Marks):
9. Describe how Philemon and Baucis welcomed the ragged strangers.
10. Why could Philemon and Baucis hardly sleep on the night the guests arrived?

IV. Extract (3 Marks):
11. "All will be well. Your kindness makes this drink a feast to us."
a) Who said this to whom? (1M)
b) What drink is referred to here? (1M)
c) What value is highlighted? (1M)

Section B: Oral Assessment (5 Marks)

• Fluency & Pronunciation (2M): Read aloud a short passage clearly.
• Comprehension (3M): Orally answer: "Why is hospitality considered an important human value?"

Key Answers

MCQs: 1. B (hut) | 2. B (Small bowl of milk) | 3. B (Refilled with milk) | 4. C (later) | 5. C (To die together)
Short Answers: 6. Strangers wore ragged clothes. | 7. Angels from heaven. | 8. Grand mansion.
Paragraphs: 9. Welcomed warmly and seated comfortably. | 10. Amazed by the refilling milk bowl.
Extract: a) Guest to Baucis. | b) Small bowl of milk. | c) Generosity and gratitude.
